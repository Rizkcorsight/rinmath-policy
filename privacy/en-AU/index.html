Purchases

Rinmath includes 3 days of full access. Starting this access period happens locally on your device and does not create a subscription or charge. After 3 days, a single optional one-time purchase unlocks the whole app permanently—there is no subscription and nothing renews or charges automatically. The App Store (iOS and macOS) or Google Play (Android) handles the purchase; Rinmath never sees your name, payment card, or billing details. The store is contacted only if you choose to buy or restore the unlock.
